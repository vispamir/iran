# history
@ Ancient Civilizations
Cities, burial grounds and excavated objects reveal Iran before the great imperial narratives. Archaeology connects these places through settlement, craft and exchange.

Carved objects and archaeological contexts invite attention to craft and exchange.|Shahr-e Sukhteh preserves traces of an early urban settlement. Buildings, burials and everyday objects illuminate life beyond royal histories.|Marlik is associated with ancient burial grounds and finely worked objects. Its material culture foregrounds metalwork and ceremonial display.|Tepe Sialk contains successive layers of ancient settlement near Kashan. Archaeological remains connect architecture, ceramics and changing forms of community.|Lorestan’s archaeological sites offer perspectives on settlement and skilled metalworking. Their local contexts matter as much as individual objects.
@ Ancient Persian Empires
Imperial history connects administration, warfare, trade and visual culture across large territories. The differences between these empires are as revealing as their shared legacies.

Court culture, rock reliefs and administrative traditions are central to its historical study.|The Medes appear in accounts of early Iranian political formations. Textual evidence and archaeology offer different approaches to their history.
